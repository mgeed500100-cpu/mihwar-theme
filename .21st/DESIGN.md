# Mihwar design context

Mihwar is a Salla Twilight theme for automotive parts and accessories. Its world is a working inspection bench rather than a cinematic car poster.

## System

- Graphite inspection surfaces sit against a warm metal paper background.
- Safety orange is the single signal color and follows the merchant primary color where Salla exposes it.
- Controls use compact square corners, visible rules, numeric indexes, and high-contrast states.
- Search and vehicle-guided discovery lead the first viewport.
- Merchant content uses native Salla components whenever possible.

## Interaction

- One inspection sweep is the signature motion and runs only once when the stage enters view.
- Routine feedback lasts 160 to 240 milliseconds.
- Motion uses transform and opacity and has a complete reduced-motion path.

## Constraints

- Arabic RTL first with complete English parity.
- No fabricated sales, availability, dispatch, authenticity, or fitment claims.
- No hover-only function, generic glow, glass cards, or decorative dashboard charts.
- Preserve Salla search, cart, checkout, account, and product behavior.
