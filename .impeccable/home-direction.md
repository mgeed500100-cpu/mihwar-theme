# Home surface

Mode: Persuade with an embedded operate task. The page must make Mihwar desirable while letting an automotive shopper begin a real search immediately.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mihwar is a live automotive test bench, not a dark car poster followed by generic ecommerce cards. The interface demonstrates diagnosis and product discovery in the first viewport.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite test-bay surfaces, warm metal whites, one safety-orange signal, thin measurement rails, calibrated ticks, squared controls with clipped inner corners, compact Arabic labels, and high-contrast numeric or status details. Avoid glow-heavy cyber styling and generic glass panels.

STORY: The shopper identifies the vehicle or enters a part name or SKU, sees the system narrow the path, then moves through categories, featured parts, brands, and service reassurance toward purchase.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-column diagnostic stage. The dominant side carries the headline and a wide functional search console. The counter-side carries a configurable vehicle image inside a measured inspection frame with three honest status labels, never fabricated statistics. On mobile, search leads and the vehicle frame becomes a compact horizontal inspection strip.

FORM: Direction 6, dynamometer inspection bench, selected from seed `e14fe9b4`. The signature interaction is a scanning measurement rail that responds once to focus and section entry, then settles. Motion uses opacity, short axis travel, and one calibrated sweep only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
