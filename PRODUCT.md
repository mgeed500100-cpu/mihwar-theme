# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Mihwar is a production Salla Twilight theme for Arabic-first automotive commerce. It is built with Twig, SCSS, Tailwind utilities, and lightweight JavaScript, and it must remain compatible with Salla storefront behavior and theme review.

## Users and jobs

- Saudi and regional automotive merchants selling spare parts, accessories, care products, oils, and filters.
- Mobile-first shoppers who need to identify a relevant product quickly, compare practical details, and buy with confidence.
- Merchants need editable sections, dependable catalog behavior, and an identity that works across different automotive specialties.

## Purpose and success

Mihwar turns a general storefront into a focused automotive catalog. Success means a shopper can search by product name or part number, browse by vehicle or category, understand availability and product details, and reach checkout without visual or interaction friction.

## Positioning

The theme should feel like a precise modern parts counter inside a premium workshop: technical, confident, and fast rather than ornamental or fashion-led. The first viewport must demonstrate product discovery, not merely show an atmospheric car image.

## Operating context

- Installed and configured through Salla Partners and the Salla theme editor.
- Source is maintained in the GitHub repository `mgeed500100-cpu/mihwar-theme`.
- Arabic RTL is primary; English LTR must remain complete and equivalent.
- Most shopping sessions are expected to begin on mobile.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Preserve native Salla search, cart, checkout, account, product, category, and notification behavior.
- Every merchant-facing content block must use supported Twilight component schemas and pass Salla validation.
- Vehicle-guided discovery may route configured choices to categories or search results. It must not claim real vehicle-to-part fitment without a dedicated database or application.
- New visible copy must be localized in Arabic and English.
- Motion must be bounded, optional under reduced-motion preferences, and must never hide essential content.
- The production build and repository must not contain credentials or fabricated commercial claims.

## Brand commitments

- Product name: `محور` in Arabic and `Mihwar` in English.
- Automotive visual language with charcoal, metal neutrals, clear white information, and a single controlled orange signal color.
- Strong search and guided discovery are central to the experience.
- References such as ReactBits, Aceternity UI, Animata, Magic UI, SupaHero, and 21st.dev are inspiration for craft and interaction only; their code and recognizable compositions are not copied.
- The rejected direction described as "style 2 mixed with style 3" is not a design brief and must not be revived as a generic hybrid.

## Evidence boundaries

No verified sales volume, dispatch time, authenticity rate, customer count, vehicle-fitment database, partner logos, or testimonials have been supplied. Demonstration content may be clearly presented as configurable sample content, but commercial proof must not be invented.

## Product principles

1. Find the right product quickly.
2. Show practical automotive detail before decorative effects.
3. Make every major block configurable by the merchant.
4. Keep purchasing behavior familiar and dependable.
5. Deliver a premium identity without sacrificing performance or accessibility.

## Accessibility baseline

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, logical RTL/LTR reading order, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, and a complete reduced-motion path.
